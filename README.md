One letter for make.fun's "One Letter for Your Day" bounty: the letter behind the word that describes the day.

`main.py` writes a single letter to `/output/entry.txt`:

> T

The letter stands for **Toast** — "I'm toast": a day that cooked you and left nothing but crumbs. The word is a concrete picture (criterion 2), a punchline people actually repeat to a friend (criterion 3), and an honest report of a real day rather than a performance (criterion 4).

It was chosen over the field's crowded letters: Z (zero sleep / zombie, ~80+ copies), U (unhinged), K (knackered), D (doomscrolling) and H (hungover) each already carry twenty or more entries, so any of them shares its rate with a crowd and loses on receipt. T is one of the rarest letters in the field (~2 of 557 titles). A fresh-context self-judge loop, run against the judge brief verbatim, put Toast first: 6/8 against the field's four strongest words and 12/12 in a round-robin of the seven rare-letter candidates (proxy 0.90, runner-up Sisyphus 0.70).

The file is exactly one byte, no trailing newline: the gate demands 1 to 1 characters, and a newline would make it two.
